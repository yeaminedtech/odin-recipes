Pilau

Description

Pilau is a fragrant rice dish popular along the East African coast. It is cooked with aromatic spices such as cumin, cardamom, cinnamon, and cloves, giving it a rich and distinctive flavor.

This recipe combines rice, meat, onions, and traditional spices to create a hearty meal that is often served during family gatherings, celebrations, and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • Cooking Oil
  • 4 Onions
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Pilau Masala

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the rice for about 20 minutes, then drain it.
  2. Slice the onions and fry them in cooking oil until they turn golden brown.
  3. Crush the garlic and ginger, then add them to the onions and cook for a minute.
  4. Add the pilau masala and stir well to release the aroma of the spices.
  5. Add the meat if using, and cook until it is well browned.
  6. Pour in water or stock and bring it to a boil.
  7. Add the rice, stir once, and reduce the heat.
  8. Cover the pot and let the pilau cook until the rice is tender and all the liquid has been absorbed.
  9. Fluff the rice with a fork and serve hot.
